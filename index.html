The platform

One system.
Three integrated applications.

Every person in your utility's ecosystem - from the MD to the meter reader in the field to the customer on their phone - operating from the same verified, real-time data. Nothing duplicated. Nothing reconciled by hand. Nothing lost.

For the utility

MAJI SaaS - the management platform.

The operational backbone of your utility. Every role, one system. Data that does not need to be extracted before it can be used.

  • MD executive dashboard - live KPIs, approval queue, WASREB benchmarks
  • WASREB-compliant IBT billing engine with full anomaly review workflow
  • M-Pesa Daraja 2.0 native - STK Push, Paybill, bank integrations
  • Automated WASREB quarterly report - MD signs, not assembles
  • Complaints management with WASREB 5-day SLA enforcement
  • WSP credit intelligence - verified operational data structured for commercial finance
For the field

The meter reading app - offline first.

Kenya's field conditions are real. The app works without connectivity. Everything captured in the field feeds directly into the billing engine the moment it syncs.

  • Full offline operation - readings, GPS coordinates, and photo evidence captured with no signal
  • Automated anomaly detection flags unusual readings before the billing cycle opens
  • Encrypted local storage - data is secure even on a lost device
  • Syncs to the platform the moment connectivity is restored - no manual upload
For the customer

MyMaji - the customer app.

Your customers get a branded, self-service experience on their smartphones. Fewer calls to your office. Faster collection. Every interaction logged against their account.

  • Live account balance, bill history, and consumption trends - always current
  • M-Pesa payments in two taps - STK Push initiated directly from the app
  • Complaint lodgement with reference number and SLA status tracking
  • Available in English and Swahili - works on any Android smartphone
